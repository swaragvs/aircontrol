Three steps, then it just works

Install the Windows app

It sits in your tray, starts with Windows, and restarts itself when needed. You never launch it by hand.

Pair once

Choose Pair new phone in the tray and scan the QR code with the AirControl app. Your PC is remembered from then on.

Hold, tilt, type

Open the app and tap your PC. Hold the pad and tilt the phone like a steering wheel to move the cursor. Tap, scroll and type from the other tabs.

What people use it for

Presentations

Click through slides and point at the screen from anywhere in the room.

Media on a big screen

Pause, skip and change the volume from the sofa when your PC is plugged into a TV.

Typing without reaching

Use your phone’s keyboard, plus Win, Alt+Tab, copy, paste and an F1 to F12 row.

Quick fixes

Control a PC with a dead touchpad or no mouse at hand.

Demos and teaching

Move around the room while you show something on screen.

Limits you should know about

Not encrypted yet

In this alpha, traffic on your Wi-Fi is not encrypted. Use it on a network you trust, like home Wi-Fi, and don’t type passwords with it.

Windows and Android only

macOS, Linux and iPhone are not supported yet.

Same Wi-Fi network

Phone and PC must be on the same network. Guest networks that isolate devices will block it.

The PC must be awake

It can’t wake a sleeping PC, and it can’t type on the lock screen or into admin (UAC) prompts.

No Fn key

Windows doesn’t let software send Fn, so AirControl gives you an F1 to F12 row instead.

Unsigned installer

Windows SmartScreen or your antivirus may warn about it. Checksums are published with every release so you can verify the file.

Scanner needs Google Play Services

On phones without it, pairing by camera won’t work. Sessions also end after 3 hours by default; change that in the tray menu.

About

What it is

A small open-source project under the MIT licence. A Python tray app on your PC receives input over your local network, and an Android app built with Capacitor sends it.

How pairing works

A one-time QR code pairs your phone. Each paired phone becomes a trusted device that you can revoke from the tray menu at any time.

Status

Early alpha, so expect rough edges. Encryption and automatic PC discovery are next on the list.
